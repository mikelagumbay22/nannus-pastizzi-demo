Nannu Means Grandpa

"Nannu" means grandpa in Maltese. Our name honours the family pastizzi tradition behind everything we bake.

Start with a Pastizz or Two

Pastizzi are Malta's favourite pastry: crisp, flaky layers wrapped around warm, savoury fillings. At Nannu's, they're made right here in our kitchen.
